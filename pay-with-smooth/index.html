01
The idea

How it works

Your website keeps doing what it already does. When it is time to take the money, you hand that one job to Smooth and get the result back.

STEP 1Your customer chooses to payOn your own checkout page, they pick Pay with Smooth alongside your other options.
STEP 2Your server asks us for a paymentOne API call with the amount and your order number. We reply with a secure payment address.
STEP 3They pay on the Smooth pageApple Pay, Google Pay, card or Tabby. The same page that already takes payments every day.
STEP 4We confirm and tell your serverThe moment the money is confirmed, we send your system a signed confirmation carrying your order number.
STEP 5Your customer is back with youThey land on whichever page of yours you chose, with the payment already recorded on your side.
What you build

A button, one call to create the payment, and one small endpoint that receives our confirmation. That is the whole integration.

What you never handle

Card numbers. They are entered on the payment page and go straight to the bank’s own secure system. They never pass through your website or your servers.

What you always control

The amount, which is set by your server and cannot be changed in anyone’s browser, and where your customer is sent afterwards.

03
Included

Four ways to pay, all from the one button

You do not choose, enable or build these separately. The page offers whichever of them the customer’s device supports, in the order most likely to be quickest for them.

Apple Apple Pay One touch on an iPhone or iPad, with no card to type. Shown automatically when the customer’s device supports it.
G Pay Google Pay The same one-tap experience on Android and on Chrome, using a card the customer has already saved with Google.
Card Credit and debit cards Visa and Mastercard, entered on the page. Where the bank requires a verification code, that step is handled inside the same flow.
tabby Pay later with Tabby The customer splits the amount into instalments with Tabby. You are paid as a single payment, exactly like the others.
Shown only when it will work

A method appears only when the customer’s device and browser can actually complete it. Nobody is offered a button that then fails, and the card option is always available as the dependable choice.

One amount, one settlement

Whichever method the customer picks, you receive one payment for the amount you asked for, recorded against your order number.

Mobile first

The payment page is built for phones and used on them every day. It works equally well opened from a desktop browser.

04
The important part

How you know it is paid

Your system is told directly, server to server. That message is the confirmation your fulfilment should act on, and it carries your own order number so it slots straight into your existing process.

Sent immediately

The moment the payment is confirmed with the bank, we send your endpoint a message containing the amount, the method used, the last four digits of the card and your order number.

Signed, so you can trust it

Every message carries a signature generated with a secret only you and Smooth hold. Your server can verify in one line that the message genuinely came from us.

Delivered reliably

If your endpoint is briefly unreachable, we keep trying on a spaced-out schedule for the next two days, and every attempt is visible to both of us.

SituationWhat happens
The customer pays normallyYou receive the confirmation within seconds, and the customer is returned to your page.
The customer closes the browser right after payingYou still receive the confirmation. It does not depend on the customer coming back to your site.
The customer decides not to payThey are returned to the page you nominated for that case, and nothing is charged.
Your server is briefly unreachableWe retry automatically, and you can also ask us for the status of any payment at any time with a single call.
Your server sends the same request twiceYou get the same payment back, not a second one. A repeated request can never produce a duplicate charge.
Also visible to you

Every payment appears in your Smooth merchant panel with its amount, method, status, time and reference, alongside the rest of your Smooth activity. So your finance team has one place to look, and nobody has to ask a developer.

05
For your developer

The integration, in full

Two calls out, one endpoint in. Everything below is what your developer needs; nothing is left to be discovered later.

5.1 The shape of it

Three moving parts 
1.  POST /api/v1/checkout/sessions      your server, when the customer chooses to pay
        → returns a checkoutUrl. Send the customer there.

2.  POST https://your-site.com/smooth/webhook
        ← we call you when the payment is confirmed. This is your proof of payment.

3.  GET  /api/v1/checkout/sessions/:id    optional, whenever you want the current state

5.2 Creating the payment

Request 
POST /api/v1/checkout/sessions
Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…              your secret key, server side only
Idempotency-Key: 4f9c1a2e-…                 any unique value per attempt
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "amount":    { "value": "125.46", "currency": "AED" },
  "reference": "4821",                        your order number, returned untouched
  "description": "Order 4821",
  "customer":  { "name": "Sara N.", "email": "sara@example.com", "phone": "+9715…" },
  "returnUrl": "https://your-site.com/order/4821/confirmed",
  "cancelUrl": "https://your-site.com/cart",
  "metadata":  { "cartId": "c_119" }              anything you like, returned as sent
}
Response 201 
{
  "id": "chk_01J8Z9Q4MF3K",
  "status": "awaiting_payment",
  "amount":    { "value": "125.46", "currency": "AED" },
  "reference": "4821",
  "checkoutUrl": "https://app.smoothpay.ai/…",   send the customer here
  "expiresAt": "2026-09-29T09:11:02Z",
  "createdAt": "2026-09-29T08:41:02Z"
}
  • The amount comes from your server and only from your server. There is no way for a browser to influence it, which is what makes the flow safe by construction.
  • The idempotency key protects you. If your request times out and you retry with the same key, you receive the original payment rather than a second one.
  • Your reference travels with the payment and comes back on the confirmation, in the status call, and on the merchant panel, so matching is exact rather than by amount and time.
  • Amounts are sent as decimal strings, so no language rounds them differently.

Reply 200 as soon as you have stored it. Anything slow or unanswered is retried.
Verifying the signature 
signed  = timestamp + "." + the raw request body
expected = HMAC_SHA256(signed, your webhook secret)

Compare expected with the v1 value, using a constant-time comparison.
Reject a timestamp more than 5 minutes old.

Verify against the raw body, before any JSON parsing or reformatting.

5.6 Security, in one place

ConcernHow it is handled
Card detailsEntered into fields supplied by the payment processor and sent directly to them. They never reach your servers or ours, which keeps your own compliance obligations at their lightest.
Your secret keyUsed server side only, never in a browser or a mobile app. It is stored hashed on our side, can be rotated at any time, and several can be active at once so a rotation needs no downtime.
The amountFixed by your server at creation. Nothing in the customer’s browser can change it.
The confirmationSigned with a shared secret and timestamped, so a message cannot be forged or replayed.
Duplicate requestsThe idempotency key guarantees one payment per attempt, however many times a request is retried.
Return addressesChecked against the addresses you registered with us, so a payment can only ever send a customer back to your own site.
TransportHTTPS throughout, in both directions.

5.7 Going live

1We send your keysA test key and a live key, plus your webhook secret, from the Smooth merchant panel.
2You build against testSame endpoints, same payloads. Test payments move no money and are kept separate from your live figures.
3One test run togetherWe watch one payment go through end to end, including your webhook, and confirm your reference matched.
4Swap one valueReplace the test key with the live key. Nothing else in your code changes.
A note your developer will appreciate

The address your customer is sent to, the request shape, the statuses and the confirmation payload are all stable. Adding a payment method or changing anything behind the scenes on our side does not alter a single field you integrate against.
